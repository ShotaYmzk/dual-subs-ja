# Store listing (English)

## Name
Dual Subs JA (PLaMo)

## Short description (max 132 characters)
Show Japanese next to Prime Video subtitles. Translation runs on your device; subtitles never leave your computer.

## Detailed description (paste as is)
Dual Subs JA adds Japanese translations right under Prime Video's English subtitles, so you can enjoy a show in English and still check the lines you missed.

PRE-TRANSLATES AHEAD OF PLAYBACK
The extension reads the subtitle file of the episode you are watching and translates the lines that are coming up, so the translation is ready when the line appears. A short line takes about a second.

RUNS ON YOUR DEVICE
Translation uses Preferred Networks' PLaMo 3 Translate, running inside your browser (WebGPU or CPU). Subtitles are never sent to a server, and nothing else has to be installed. (If you already use Ollama, you can pick it instead.)

BUILT FOR LEARNING ENGLISH
- Bilingual view (English + Japanese), Japanese only, or English only
- Hover any English word to see its meaning and a short phrase translation (playback pauses while you read)
- A transcript panel; click a line to jump to that scene
- Auto-pause after every line; keys A / S / D for previous / replay / next
- A glossary to fix the translation of names and terms
- Show subtitles over the picture, or in a bar below it

NO ENGLISH SUBTITLES?
If a title only has Japanese subtitles, the extension translates them into English and shows both.

HOW TO USE
1. After installing, a setup page opens. Download the translation model once (about 573 MB).
2. Start a title on Prime Video with English subtitles on (or Japanese if English is not available).
3. Use the "JA" button next to Prime Video's subtitle button to open the transcript.

PRIVACY
Subtitle text is processed on your computer only to translate it. It is not sent anywhere or stored. No analytics, no ads. The only network access is downloading the translation model from Hugging Face.

NOTES
- Translations are machine-generated by PLaMo 3 Translate and can contain mistakes.
